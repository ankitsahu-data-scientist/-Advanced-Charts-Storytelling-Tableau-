## Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Electronics Category Dominance

* Electronics generated the highest sales among all categories.
* It contributed the largest share of overall business revenue.
* Profit margins remained competitive despite high sales volume.

### 2. Top Performing Cities

* A few cities contributed a significant portion of total sales.
* Business performance is concentrated in key urban markets.

### 3. Star Product Analysis

* Certain products achieved both high sales and high profit.
* These products act as major revenue drivers for the business.

### 4. Revenue Trend

* Sales showed a generally positive trend over time.
* Revenue growth indicates increasing customer demand and business expansion.

### 5. Discount Impact Analysis

* Higher discounts did not always result in higher profits.
* Several heavily discounted products generated lower profitability.
* A balanced discount strategy is important to maximize profit.

### 6. High Value Products

* Products with sales above ₹10,000 contributed a major portion of total revenue.
* Identifying high-value products helps prioritize inventory and marketing efforts.

### Business Recommendation

* Focus marketing efforts on Electronics and top-performing cities.
* Promote star products to maximize revenue.
* Optimize discount policies to improve profitability.
* Monitor high-value products closely for sustained growth.
